About this prototype

  • This is a demonstration for review. It is not a medical device, and the starter words have not yet been checked by a speech-language therapist.
  • Everything stays on this tablet: no account, no tracking, no recordings, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Opened from its web link and added to the home screen, the board keeps working without internet. Opened as a file, it works offline only while the tab stays open. The voice comes from the tablet itself; voices marked "on this device" usually work offline.
  • The app never guesses how a child feels. A feeling is only spoken when the child chooses it.
  • Yes, No, Stop, Help and Break are always on screen and never move.
  • Adults reach this page by holding the round button in the top corner for 3 seconds, or tapping it 4 times quickly. On a keyboard, hold Enter on it or press Ctrl + Shift + S.
